Schaefer DouglasA, McDowell WH, Scatena FN, Asbury CE. Effects of hurricane disturbance on stream water concentrations and fluxes in eight tropical forest watersheds of the Luquillo Experimental Forest, Puerto Rico. *Journal of Tropical Ecology.* 2000;16(2):189-207. 10.1017/S0266467400001358

McDowell, William H., and USDA Forest Service. International Institute Of Tropical Forestry (IITF). 2024. “Chemistry of Stream Water from the Luquillo Mountains.” [Dataset] *Environmental Data Initiative.* https://doi.org/10.6073/PASTA/F31349BEBDC304F758718F4798D25458.
